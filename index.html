Historical Model Accuracy

Walk-forward evaluation using only games before each prediction. Research models, not the live v1.5 projection formula.

MAE = mean absolute error; RMSE penalizes larger misses; bias = projection minus actual. Historical sample excludes players with fewer than 3 previous games and does not evaluate DNPs. Counts are player-game observations for each stat; do not sum across stats. The underlying data includes historical ACC players and selected transfers, so coverage is not a random Division I sample.

Next research step: directly backtest the live projection algorithm, then test opponent pace, defensive efficiency, and sportsbook implied team totals against the same baseline.

KenPom Ratings & CBBD Comparison

Opponent-adjusted ratings for all Division I teams. Saved snapshots remain local to this browser unless exported.

Loading KenPom ratings…

KenPom AdjO and AdjD are adjusted points per 100 possessions; AdjT is adjusted possessions per game. Raw CBBD figures are not directly comparable estimates. Positive/negative gaps are descriptive, not prediction errors. KenPom values are not yet used in player projections.
